---
title: "SIB-200"
description: "Why sib200-eu runs 10-shot with the first 10 train examples, scored with acc."
---

`sib200-eu` runs 10-shot since [#137](https://github.com/OpenEuroLLM/oellm-eval/pull/137). Before that it ran 0-shot, and few-shot runs took their examples from the test split.

| Choice | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Shots | 10 | 0-shot scores low and jumps between checkpoints. 10 shots score higher and move less on both models. |
| `fewshot_split` | `train` | The test split is sorted by label and starts with 51 science/technology items, so its first examples all carry that label. |
| `fewshot_config.sampler` | `first_n` | The first 10 train examples beat 10 random train or validation examples, and every item in every language gets the same prompt. |
| Metric | `acc` | `acc_norm` divides by label length and scores 0.25 at 0-shot, the same as always answering science/technology. It also stays below `acc` at 10 shots. |

| Setup | 2B mean | 2B change | 9B mean | 9B change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0-shot (before #137) | 0.367 | 0.066 | 0.438 | 0.093 |
| train, first 5 | 0.525 | 0.075 | 0.770 | 0.050 |
| train, first 10 (used) | 0.755 | 0.045 | 0.817 | 0.030 |
| train, random 10 | 0.649 | 0.045 | 0.787 | 0.027 |
| validation, random 10 | 0.674 | 0.036 | 0.800 | 0.041 |
| test, first 10 | 0.250 | 0.000 | 0.282 | 0.027 |

"Mean" is over ten checkpoints of [`openeurollm/datamix-2b-80-20`](https://huggingface.co/openeurollm/datamix-2b-80-20) (2B, steps 100k to 950k) and [`openeurollm/prelude`](https://huggingface.co/openeurollm/prelude) (9B, steps 100,800 to 950,400, before cooldown). "Change" is the mean absolute change between consecutive checkpoints. Random setups draw a new set per test item with lm-eval's default seed (1234). Runs used oellm-eval 0.4.0b1, lm-eval 0.4.13 and the full test set of 204 items per language.

Always answering science/technology scores 51/204 = 0.25. That is above chance, so the old few-shot plateau looked like a weak model.

## Caveats

- Five of the 36 languages. On the first 2B checkpoint, where all 36 ran, the setups rank in the same order.
- The first 10 train examples have no travel or sports items, which are 65 of the 204 test items. Accuracy per label was not checked.
- The 9B model is near 0.8 at 10 shots from its first checkpoint on, so SIB-200 shows little change across Prelude's training.

<Accordion title="Reproduce">
  Each setup is a task YAML next to a copy of the bundled `_default_template_yaml`. The `sib200an_` prefix keeps the names apart from the bundled tasks.

  ```yaml
  include: _default_template_yaml
  task: sib200an_trainfirst_fin_Latn
  dataset_name: fin_Latn
  test_split: test
  fewshot_split: train
  fewshot_config:
    sampler: first_n
  ```

  List one row per checkpoint, task and shot count. `model_path` is a local snapshot of a checkpoint branch, for example from `snapshot_download("openeurollm/prelude", revision="iter_0950400")`.

  ```csv
  model_path,task_path,n_shot,eval_suite
  /path/to/prelude/iter_0950400,sib200an_trainfirst_fin_Latn,10,lm-eval-harness
  ```

  ```bash
  oellm-eval schedule --eval_csv_path evals.csv --lm_eval_include_path tasks/
  oellm-eval collect --results_dir <run directory> --output_csv results.csv --check true
  ```
</Accordion>
